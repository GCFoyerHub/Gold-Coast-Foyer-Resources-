Connect your Google Calendar

1. Open Google Calendar → click the three dots next to your calendar name on the left.

2. Click Settings → scroll down to "Secret address in iCal format" → copy that URL.

3. Open this HTML file, find the line const ICAL_URL = ''; near the top of the script, and paste your URL between the quotes. Save and re-upload — done.
