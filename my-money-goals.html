Get started

  1. Download the planner and save it to your OneDrive
    iPhone: if it opens a preview, tap Share, then Save to Files (choose OneDrive) or Open in Excel.
    Android: it lands in your Downloads. Tap to open it in Excel, then Save a copy to OneDrive.
  2. Get the free Excel app on your phone
  3. Share it with your worker
  4. Type in the yellow boxes only. The rest works itself out
Working with your key worker

Your coach, not your accountant

Your worker is here to back you, not to run your money for you. You bring the effort, they bring the support. That's The Deal.

Bring to your catch-up

  • 📱Your planner, open on your phone
  • 🏦Your banking app, so you can add real balances
  • ❓One money question or worry, big or small

What you'll do together

  • ⭐Check your goal bar and what's moved since last time
  • 🏆Name one win, even a small one
  • 👣Set one next step for the coming fortnight

Ask your worker about

  • 💰Opening a separate savings account
  • 🎓Saver Plus or the certified Be MoneySmart course
  • 🧾Centrelink, bills or debt you're stuck on
  • 🧭EPCS and getting ready for 21, if it applies to you
Your planner is yours. Your worker only sees it if you share it, and nobody is here to judge. Red numbers are just information, and something to work on together.
