What it means

The Foyer
Deal

The Foyer Deal fosters mutual responsibility by clearly defining roles and accountability between young people and the staff who support them.

It helps young people invest in their lives and community, strengthening ties and relationships that last beyond their time at the Foyer.

This isn't a one-way arrangement. Both sides show up. Both sides commit. That's what makes it work.

Your commitment

The Foyer 3D

D1 🔭
Discover

Look outward. Explore what's possible. Identify your skills, strengths and potential — and move from simply coping to actively thriving.

D2 ⚙️
Dedication

Dedication is about showing up for your goals — even when it's hard. It means making a clear decision about what you're working toward and staying the course through setbacks, distractions, and doubt.

D3 💪
Discipline

Discipline is about what you do every day — the routines, boundaries and choices that quietly build the life you want. It's less about motivation and more about the systems you create to keep moving forward.

What's on the table

The 6
Offers

Grounded in social theories of change, the 6 Offers represent the areas where the Foyer actively partners with you — the real, practical support available when you're ready to engage with it.

🎓
Education

Access pathways to learning — from completing school to vocational training, university, and everything in between.

💼
Employment

Build your career story. Get support finding work, developing skills, and creating sustainable income.

🏠
Housing & Living Skills

Learn how to manage a home, budget, cook, and sustain independent living well beyond the Foyer.

🤝
Social Connections

Build relationships that matter — with peers, mentors, and the wider community around you.

🏘️
Community Participation

Get involved, contribute, and belong. Community is built through presence and participation.

❤️
Health & Wellbeing

Physical, mental and emotional health are the foundation of everything. Access support to look after yourself.
